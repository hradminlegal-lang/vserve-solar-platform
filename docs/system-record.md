# Homeowner system record and service

Every system installed through the platform gets a record that belongs to the homeowner, not the installer. If the installer stops answering or closes down, the owner still has the documents and a way to get service.

## What the record holds
- System summary: address, kWp, panel/inverter/battery models, layout image, single-line diagram, design report (signed version).
- Assets: each panel, inverter, battery and optimiser with serial number, supplier, purchase date, and the warranty terms on file (product, performance, workmanship) with expiry dates.
- Documents: contract, commissioning test record, CFEI, utility and net-metering papers, handover pack, receipts.
- Installer details at the time of install (company, licensed engineer, contacts).
- Production log: monthly kWh entered by the owner or read from the inverter app later; compared with the design's expected production.
- Service history: every ticket, visit, part replaced (old and new serials), photos.

## Ownership and sharing
- Created automatically at project handover; the owner confirms it.
- Owner controls access: the original installer keeps read access by default; the owner can grant or revoke access for any other company or technician (with expiry).
- Owners can export the whole record as PDF + ZIP at any time.
- Systems installed outside the platform can be added by the owner (upload documents, scan serial labels) and marked "Owner-entered".

## Service requests
1. Owner reports a problem or books maintenance: category (no production, inverter error, leak, damage, cleaning, inspection), photos, error codes.
2. The platform shows who is responsible on paper: original installer workmanship warranty, product warranty with supplier/manufacturer, or none. It is information only; warranty decisions stay with the installer, supplier and manufacturer under their terms.
3. Routing: first to the original installer (respond within 48 h); if no answer, or the owner chooses, to verified technicians nearby through the maintenance app's booking flow.
4. Work recorded: findings, parts replaced with serials, photos, owner approval; record updated.
5. Warranty claim packet: one click builds a PDF with serials, purchase proof, install and commissioning documents, fault photos for the owner or technician to send to the warranty holder.

## Rules
- No promise of warranty coverage by VServe.
- Serial numbers validated for format and duplicates; a serial already registered on another system is flagged for admin review (possible grey-market or fake item).
- Personal data minimal; documents private to the owner and those they share with.
